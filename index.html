SECTION 1: Reading Comprehension

Reading Text: A House in the Trees

Ben Miller is 13. He’s from Texas in the USA. There are a lot of people in his family – his mum and dad, his brothers, Tom and Brad, and his sisters, Kim and Nancy.

The Millers’ new house has got an amazing garden with lots of trees. In one of the trees, there is a big treehouse. It’s got a bedroom, a bathroom, a living room and a small kitchen. There are two sofas in the living room, but there isn’t a TV. There are some stairs. There’s also a balcony with beautiful views. It’s really cool!

1. Ben Miller is...
2. There are ________ children in the Miller family.

Task 2: Read again. Answer in full sentences.

1. How old is Ben?
2. Where is Ben from?
3. What are the names of Ben’s sisters?
4. Is there a bathroom in the treehouse?
5. Would you like a treehouse in your garden? Why or why not?
